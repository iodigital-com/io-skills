# Prepare a release

Agree on rollout and recovery before a feature goes into production. Decide who will monitor the first users and when the team should act.

## Example prompt

> We want to roll out the updated mobile comparison gradually. What signals confirm that the selected bike light reaches the basket? Describe how we can restore the previous view if a problem appears.

## What do you get?

A release checklist, rollout sequence and signals for continuing or stopping. The rollback plan records actions and responsibilities in case the first group reports problems.
