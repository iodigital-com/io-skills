# Refine an idea

Explore different directions when the problem is familiar but the team is still considering which solution is worth trying.

## Example prompt

> After a project meeting, team members often don’t know who will act on a decision. Explore three ways to make that clear. Help me choose one idea to try at our next meeting and identify the assumption it would test.

## What do you get?

A concept with a problem statement, a recommended direction and a scoped first version. Its assumptions and open questions guide the next experiment.
