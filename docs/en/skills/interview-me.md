# Clarify the underlying request

Clarify a brief when the same request could mean different things to different people. The skill asks one question at a time and shares its working interpretation.

## Example prompt

> Our bike shop asks for “product comparison”. Ask me about a cyclist in the shop deciding between two models. Find out what they weigh up when choosing a light, bag or lock and what they want to do next. Ask one question at a time.

## What do you get?

A jointly confirmed statement of the audience, intended outcome and boundaries of the request. It explains how the people involved will recognise a successful solution.
