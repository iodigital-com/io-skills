# Set measurable quality requirements

Agree on measurable requirements when words such as fast or accessible mean different things to people on a project.

## Example prompt

> We want the bike light comparison to become usable quickly on a phone. Help me choose a measurable threshold, a representative test device and a repeatable check for changes to this screen.

## What do you get?

A `CONSTRAINTS.md` records agreed thresholds, how to check them and how to document exceptions. A diff check flags changes such as skipped tests and lowered thresholds.
